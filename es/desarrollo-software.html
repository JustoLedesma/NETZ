What it does

Four things between your webhook sender and your app.

Type inference on ingest

Relay computes the shape of every event stream as payloads arrive. As schemas drift, Relay spots new optional fields and updates the union without breaking your handler.

  • Zero-config TypeScript, Go and Python definitions
  • Breaking changes flagged before they hit your queue
  • Historic payloads re-typed on schema migration

Exponential backoff with jitter

If your destination returns 5xx or drops the connection, Relay holds the line. Retries run up to 72 hours with exponential backoff and jitter to protect cold destinations.

  • Automatic 5xx and timeout retries up to 72 hours
  • Circuit breaker opens if your receiver goes down
  • Per-source concurrency limits to prevent thundering herds

One-click replays from any point

Shipped a bug in your webhook consumer? Fix it, deploy, and replay the last six hours of events directly to your receiver. Relay preserves the original headers and IDs.

  • Replay by time window, provider, or individual event ID
  • Dry-run replays locally through Relay's CLI tunnel
  • Immutable archive kept 30 days on every plan

Provider signatures verified at the gate

Relay verifies Stripe, GitHub, Shopify and Svix signatures before an event touches your servers. Bad signatures are rejected at the edge and never reach your quota.

  • Pre-configured signature verification for 40+ providers
  • Zero secrets inside your app handlers
  • Replay attacks dropped via timestamp threshold checks

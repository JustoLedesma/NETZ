  • What is a domain name?

    A domain name is the unique, exclusive digital address that identifies your website on the Internet (such as netz.com.py or yourbrand.com). It allows visitors to access your online presence easily without having to memorize complex numerical IP addresses.

  • How does a domain name work?

    A domain acts as a pointer directed to the web server that hosts your website files and databases. When a visitor types your domain into a browser, global Domain Name Servers (DNS) translate the name into your server's IP address and deliver the webpage immediately.

  • Looking for regional Paraguay (.py) domains? (.com.py, .net.py, .org.py)

    Yes! We register and manage regional ccTLDs including .com.py, .net.py, and .org.py. If you need specialized documentation assistance for institutional extensions (.edu.py, .gov.py), our support team will handle manual verification and setup on your behalf.

  • Does migrating or transferring a domain have any cost?

    Pointing your existing domain nameservers to our hosting platform is 100% free. For registrar transfers (gTLDs like .com, .net), standard ICANN renewal fees apply, which extends your domain expiration date by one full additional year.

  • How do I migrate my domain to NETZ?

    To migrate your domain, simply update its nameservers (DNS) at your current registrar to our high-speed nameservers: ns1.netz.com.py and ns2.netz.com.py. DNS propagation typically completes across worldwide networks within 2 to 24 hours.

  • What is Web Hosting?

    Web Hosting is the physical and virtual server infrastructure where your website’s files, code, databases, images, and email accounts reside. When someone enters your domain name, the hosting server delivers those files to the user's browser securely and instantly.

  • What are professional corporate emails?

    Professional corporate emails are email addresses that utilize your own custom domain (such as contact@yourcompany.com) instead of generic providers like Gmail or Yahoo. They reinforce brand credibility, include webmail access, spam protection, and sync seamlessly with mobile devices and desktop mail clients.

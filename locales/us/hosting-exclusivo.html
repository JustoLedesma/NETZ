Frequently Asked QuestionsIf your questions are not answered here, feel free to visit our Help Center & FAQ section
O escribenos en cualquiera through our 24/7 customer support channels.
Are web hosting plans managed?

NETZ web hosting is semi-managed. We handle core server maintenance, hardware, network security, and OS updates, while giving you full intuitive control of your websites and emails via cPanel.

Do I need to purchase a domain when ordering a plan?

No es necesario comprar un dominio para contratar un plan. Puedes trabajar con tu proveedor o transferir el dominio con nosotros.

How long does account activation take?

Account activation is initiated immediately once payment confirmation is received by our gateway.

Can I switch to another plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time seamlessly from your client dashboard without downtime.

What database types are included?

Las bases de datos disponibles en nuestros planes de Hosting incluyen: bases de datos MySQL y PostgreSQL bajo MariaDB.

What is the service uptime guarantee?

Our service uptime guarantee is 99.9%, backed by robust enterprise infrastructure and 24/7 proactive monitoring.

What content is permitted?

All services provided by NETZ must be used for lawful purposes. Malware, phishing, unsolicited spam, and copyright-infringing content are strictly prohibited.

Do you provide SSH access?

We provide secure SSH access on all web hosting plans for verified users upon request.

SSH access is intended for experienced developers. We do not provide technical support or custom debugging for bash terminal commands, scripts, or server environment changes executed via SSH.

Do you support Ruby, Python, and Node.js?

We support Node.js, Python, and Ruby on all web hosting plans via cPanel CloudLinux App Selector. These application runtimes require developer knowledge; custom programming support is not provided for third-party scripts.

Is sending spam permitted?

Sending unsolicited bulk emails or spam is strictly prohibited and results in immediate account suspension.

Can I create backups?

Puedes realizar copias de seguridad manuales desde cPanel. Recuerda eliminar la copia de backups once downloaded to your local drive. Hosting persistent full-server permanente ninguna copia de seguridad.

What is our security level?

We deploy enterprise-grade defense layers including real-time BitNinja WAF, automated DDoS mitigation, malware scanning, and isolated container environments.

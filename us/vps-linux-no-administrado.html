What is an Unmanaged Linux VPS Server?

NETZ Unmanaged Linux VPS servers provide full root SSH credentials to your cuenta y control total sobre las configuraciones de su servidor. Esta es una excelente cost-effective solution if you do not require server administration support and feel confident monitoring and managing your server stack independently. The low cost of these unmanaged tiers managing your own operating system. It is designed for developers and system administrators with server management experience. Unmanaged VPS plans do not include operating system technical support.

Can I upgrade my unmanaged server to fully managed?

NETZ allows you to upgrade to Managed administration services at any time if you decide que necesita asistencia o soporte para su VPS no administrado. Esto se puede hacer en any time directly through your cloud control panel with 1 click. Once upgraded, our certified engineering team takes over technical maintenance, OS updates, and 24/7 proactive monitoring.

What is the difference between Unmanaged Linux and Managed Linux?
Linux completamente administrados?

Un VPS completamente administrado viene con soporte 24/7/365. En otras palabras, si submits a request, our engineers will actively handle server-side issues. For example, if your OS needs security patches, our team takes care of it. With an Unmanaged VPS, NETZ ensures hardware uptime, network routing, and power redundancy, while internal software configuration remains the sole responsibility of the client.

What are the benefits of choosing an Unmanaged Linux VPS?

Si eres un desarrollador de sitios web, un administrador de sistemas o tiene su propio negocio listo para la nube con un equipo de TI ya establecido, los servidores no Unmanaged VPS instances provide full administrative root access, SSH, and VNC web console at affordable rates. NETZ Cloud Control grants you complete freedom to configure, reboot, reinstall, and optimize your virtual server environment.

Should I choose a Managed or Unmanaged Linux VPS?

The choice depends entirely on your operational needs. If you possess server administration experience and require full root freedom to deploy custom stacks, Unmanaged is the most cost-effective choice. If you prefer our certified team to handle maintenance, monitoring, and updates, Managed VPS is the ideal solution.
